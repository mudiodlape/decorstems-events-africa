## Brand & Style
The brand embodies timeless romantic luxury, botanical grandeur, and bespoke celebration culture across East Africa. It speaks to discerning couples, corporate institutions, and patrons of refined gatherings who seek elevated, unforgettable experiences.

The design movement is **Editorial Luxury Minimalism**, marked by generous whitespace, deliberate asymmetry, poetic typography, and tactile restraint. The visual atmosphere is calm, prestigious, and deeply atmospheric—reminiscent of architectural publications and haute couture lookbooks. It eschews loud gradients, frenetic micro-interactions, and dense data layouts in favor of statuesque portraiture, quiet borders, and balanced proportion.

## Colors
The palette evokes the lush landscapes of the Great Rift Valley paired with warm equatorial sunlight and gilded brass craft.

- **Primary (`#1B3B2B`) & Deep Botanical (`#142E21`):** Serves as the anchor for primary call-to-actions, focal headlines, and strong brand moments.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059`) & Gold Highlights (`#DFBA73`, `#A6823B`):** Reserved for delicate divider rules, editorial labels, status badges, and refined hairline frames. Never used as heavy solid background fills for large containers.
- **Canvas & Neutral Surfaces:** The foundation is warm ivory (`#FAF8F5`) transitioning to pale linen (`#F5F1EA`) for layered contrast. Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) is reserved strictly for elevated card surfaces, photo framing, and modal dialogs.
- **Typography & Boundaries:** Deep charcoal (`#1C1C1C`) for primary reading, muted stone charcoal (`#5A5A5A`) for secondary editorial annotations, with hairlines structured in pale sand (`#E8E2D8`) or translucent gold (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.2)`).

## Typography
Typographic rhythm relies on high-contrast pairings: the stately, calligraphic grace of Playfair Display set against the airy, geometric precision of Plus Jakarta Sans.

- **Display & Headlines:** Maintain an unhurried, editorial character. Use generous line height to allow the serifs room to breathe.
- **Labels & Sub-headers:** Rendered in small, tracked-out uppercase (`letterSpacing: 0.12em` to `0.16em`) to echo luxury catalog folios and curatorial captions.
- **Body Copy:** Maintained at regular to light weights (`300` to `400`) to sustain an airy visual density that contrasts cleanly against bold imagery.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model employs a fluid 12-column editorial grid configured for cinematic pacing and generous margins.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Centered max-width container capped at 1440px with `4rem` outer canvas margins and `2rem` column gutters. Layouts interleave full-bleed hero visuals with staggered 5-column and 7-column content pairings.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `2.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Multi-column editorial compositions simplify into balanced 4-column blocks.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `1.5rem` canvas margins and `1rem` gutters. Full vertical stacking with increased vertical section separation (`3rem` to `4rem`) to preserve the feeling of uncrowded space.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through tonal layering, crisp borders, and soft ambient light rather than aggressive drop shadows.

- **Tonal Tiers:** Base layer (`#FAF8F5`) holds the background canvas. Elevated cards rest on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or pale sand (`#F5F1EA`), delimited by a 1px border of `#E8E2D8` or `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.25)`.
- **Ambient Shadows:** When hovering or raising overlays (modals, drawers, selected portfolio previews), use soft, warm-tinted dispersion:
  - *Resting:* `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(28, 28, 28, 0.04)`
  - *Floating / Card Hover:* `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(27, 59, 43, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.06)`
- **Divider Rules:** Subtle horizontal rules (`1px solid #E8E2D8`) or tapered champagne accents (`linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, #C5A059, transparent)`) establish structure without visual clutter.

## Shapes
The structural language leans architectural and tailored:

- **Cards, Panels & Containers:** Subtle softenings using `roundedness: 1` (4px / `0.25rem` up to 8px / `0.5rem` on larger modals). Sharp, clean corners convey discipline and gallery-like craftsmanship.
- **Exceptions for Metadata & Pills:** Badges, tags, filter chips, and tertiary button capsules strictly use pill profiles (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to contrast with geometric image frames and rectangular layouts.
- **Imagery:** Media frames should be strict geometric rectangles (4:5 vertical portraits or 16:9 cinematic landscapes) with minimal 4px radius edges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid deep emerald (`#1B3B2B`) background with ivory (`#FAF8F5`) text. Padding: `14px 28px`. Border-radius: `4px`. Hover: transitions to deep forest (`#142E21`) with subtle lift.
- **Secondary (Editorial Outline):** Transparent background with a `1px` border of deep emerald (`#1B3B2B`) or champagne gold (`#C5A059`). Text matches border color in uppercase tracking (`label-lg`). Hover fills with a translucent champagne wash (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.08)`).
- **Text Link / Action:** Lowercase or uppercase serif link accompanied by a fine horizontal rule that expands on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Editorial Badges:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `6px 14px`. Subtle background in warm off-white (`#FAF8F5`), bounded by a `1px` border of champagne gold (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.4)`). Typography: uppercase `label-md` in deep charcoal or emerald.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Fields:** Minimalist presentation. Crisp white background (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px` base border in `#E8E2D8`. Padding: `14px 16px`. Focus state shifts the border to deep emerald (`#1B3B2B`) or gold (`#C5A059`) with zero harsh outer glow. Labels sit above in `label-md` uppercase stone charcoal (`#5A5A5A`).

### Cards & Event Showcases
- **Portfolio & Service Cards:** White canvas (`#FFFFFF`), `1px` border in `#E8E2D8`, internal padding set to `space-lg` (`2.5rem`). Photography sits flush or within a padded matte frame. Hover states subtly lift the card with the warm ambient emerald-tinted shadow.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Custom circular radios and refined square checkboxes with `2px` soft radius. Borders styled in `#C5A059`. Checked state fills with deep emerald (`#1B3B2B`) featuring a clean white check icon.

### Dividers & Flourishes
- Section breaks feature a centered 48px hairline gold bar (`#C5A059`) or a single floral leaf glyph set in gold between two extending sand (`#E8E2D8`) rules.
